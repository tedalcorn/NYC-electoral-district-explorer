DISTRICT PORTAL — BUILD PROMPT
NYU Senior Seminar (Fall 2026) · pilot for NY State Senate District 20 (Sen. Zellnor Myrie)
Last revised: 2026-07-15

WHAT THIS IS
A one-page dashboard that collates publicly available information about a single
legislative district, so students can find it fast and then confirm it at the source.
It is a starting point for reporting, never a citation.

GROUND RULES (apply to every pane)
- No invented data. A failed fetch shows an honest gap, never a guess or an interpolation.
- Every pane names its source, the retrieval date, and what could be wrong with it.
- Prefer primary sources (Census API, Board of Elections, NYC Open Data) over aggregators.
- Charts start their y-axis at zero. Category and bracket choices are disclosed.
- The page is a claim, not a fact. It says so, and tells the reader to cite the origin.

WHAT THE PAGE CONTAINS
1. Map of the district (Census TIGER/Line, 2022 lines) with theme overlays: community districts;
   criminal justice (precincts + shooting incidents); transit (subway lines in MTA colors, bus routes,
   Citi Bike); evictions; income and demographics by census tract; schools and early education;
   licensed food stores. Each layer states its date range and how many records could not be mapped.
2. Who are residents — population, median household income, share foreign-born, median age,
   each shown against the citywide figure. Source: ACS 5-year (2020-24).
3. Census detail — race/ethnicity and age vs. NYC; population and median income over time,
   with the 2022 redistricting break marked. Source: ACS 5-year via the Census API.
4. Community districts — the NYC community boards that overlap the legislative district, each
   with its self-identified needs and links to the source. Also a map theme: the community-district
   boundaries overlaid on the district, each clickable to jump to its card below.
5. Background on the member — Wikipedia, election history with vote totals, signature priorities.
6. Local news — last 6 months, news organizations only, coverage of the same event grouped into one
   story, tagged by topic, filterable.
7. Resources — primary-source portals for going deeper.

HOW IT'S BUILT
A single Python script (build/build_portal.py) fetches each source, writes one JSON file per
pane (each with a provenance block), and bakes them into data/portal_data.js so the page runs
from a static file or GitHub Pages. index.html is presentation only.

COLLECTING COMMUNITY-DISTRICT NEEDS (reproducible from public sources — no private email needed)
Everything below is public; an agent can assemble it from web search and open data alone.
1. Find which community districts overlap the legislative district. Boundaries: NYC Open Data
   "Community Districts" (dataset 5crt-au7u, GeoJSON export); field boro_cd = borough digit + CD
   number (Brooklyn = 3xx). Intersect with the legislative-district polygon; keep the CDs that
   overlap. Simplify and bake to data/community_districts.js as window.CD_GEO (property cd).
2. For each community district, pull its NYC Dept. of City Planning Community Profile:
   communityprofiles.planning.nyc.gov/<borough>/<N> — standardized demographics and indicators.
3. Find that board's "Statement of Community District Needs" for the latest fiscal year. It is a
   public PDF. Search e.g.  "Community Board <N>" Brooklyn "Statement of Community District Needs"
   FY2027 filetype:pdf  — or the board's official site (cb<N>brooklyn.* / nyc.gov/site/brooklyncb<N>).
   The consolidated set is also published centrally by NYC OMB (the annual "Community District
   Needs" / register of community-board budget requests). Prefer the board's own current-year PDF.
4. From each statement, extract the board's TOP THREE PRESSING ISSUES plus its highest-ranked
   capital/expense requests. Distill each to a short phrase; do not reproduce the whole document.
   Read past a board that reframes the form — pull its substantive priorities anyway (e.g. CB14's
   park-access and housing-condition needs sit beneath a governance critique).
5. Provenance and honesty: each card links the community profile + the statement PDF and names the
   fiscal year. If a board has not posted a current statement, say so and link the board instead —
   never guess its priorities. Flag an older-year statement as such.
This static, human-checked content is embedded in index.html (const CDS) rather than fetched live,
since it changes about once a year, not daily.
