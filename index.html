NYC Electoral District Explorer

This is a pilot dashboard built with AI assistance. It is meant to quickly collate available information on the district, not as the source of that information. Treat each piece of information on it as a claim rather than a fact and confirm them directly with the primary source — DO NOT CITE THIS PAGE. Each visualization and field includes the name and URL of its source, when it was retrieved, and considerations about the data. The prompts used to build it are summarized here.
